How I work
  1. 01

    A short message

    Two or three lines: the business, its scale, the question you need answered

  2. 02

    The question, the data, the first step. The conversation is confidential

  3. 03

    Scope, deliverables, roles, timeline and fee, fixed in writing

  4. 04

  5. 05

  2. Embedded workstream leadPart of your team, running the work from the insideFrom 2 months, 2–3 days a week
  3. AdvisorRegular reviews of decisions with the ownerTwo sessions a month
  4. Single-question reviewOne calculation or recommendation5–7 working days

Timelines are typical; the exact one comes after the call. Never more than two engagements at a time. With startups, payment can be equity or an option
